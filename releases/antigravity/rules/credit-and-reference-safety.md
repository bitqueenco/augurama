# Video generation safety

Never spend generation credits through an autonomous hook, subagent, background retry, shell command, or inferred approval. Use the direct-video workflow. Human approval applies to one exact frozen contract, not an unlimited iteration loop.

Keep provider secrets in the secure service account page. Do not scrape Dreamina cookies or make local/private media public. Declare likeness and permissions truthfully. Do not claim provider accounts, entitlements, credits, cancellation, refunds, exact continuity or a generated result without evidence.

A background task may check a known generation ID and report its result. It must not initiate a replacement. `submission_unknown` is an operator-reconciliation state, not a retry signal.
